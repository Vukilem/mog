Questions

Good to know

Is MOG free?

The prep helper on this page is free, with no sign-up. The app includes a free tier for schedules and logging, with an optional upgrade for reminders, multi-compound stacks, and history export.

What can I track?

Medication schedules, dose entries, injection sites, and treatment history across one or more compounds. The prep helper works with any compound you reconstitute with bacteriostatic or sterile water.

Is my data private?

Your logs are yours. MOG doesn't sell treatment data, and everything you track stays tied to your account only.

Does MOG give medical advice?

No. MOG is a personal logbook for schedules and treatment history — not a clinical tool. Always follow guidance from your clinician for any medication protocol.

What devices does it run on?

The prep helper works in any modern browser, desktop or mobile. The MOG app is currently in build for iOS.
